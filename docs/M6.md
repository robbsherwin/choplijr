# M6 — hostages

`DESIGN.md` section 13: spawn, AI states, boarding, unloading, rescue
counter. This program does that and stops. Fire is still a stub (M7). Not
tanks, jets, bullets, or the type 1/2 death cycle. Not the game.

M5 put the original helicopter on the M4 world. M6 is that flight plus
hostages from `choplifter.s`. Attended runs open on the same BIOS mode 3
title as M5, then mode 8.

1. **Spawn.** 64 hostages, 16 in each of four barracks. The right-most house
   starts on fire and dumps eight into the field (`spawnInitialHostages`).
   Further field spawns come from burning houses only, every fourth sim tick,
   while fewer than five are active and the 16-slot table is not full.
   Intact barracks keep their 16 until M7 tanks set them alight.
2. **AI.** Waving (4-frame cycle, three art frames ping-pong) or running left
   / right at 2 world pixels per tick. Landed in the field with room aboard:
   they run toward the skids. No Playdate rope. Airborne they mostly mill and
   wave. On the pad they run right to `DOOR_X`.
3. **Boarding / capacity 16.** Fully landed, not dying, left of `BASE_X_H`,
   within the original 8–9 px (right) / 10–11 px (left) window: they board.
   Closer than that is a crush (killed++). A full cabin (`HOSTAGES_LOADED`
   = 16) turns them away. Boarding animation then frees the slot; they are
   already counted aboard.
4. **Unload.** Land on the base pad (`landed_base`). Each spawn tick peels
   one off the cabin, increments rescued, and sends them running to
   `DOOR_X` (4821). `CURR_LEVEL` ticks once per unload session (M7 reads it).
5. **HUD rows 0–7.** `K nn  A nn  R nn` (killed / aboard / rescued). F1 or
   `` ` `` swaps the M5 debug line; that stays **off** by default.
6. **Reuse.** JrConfig pages, `blit_rle`, even-width dirty lists, M4 camera,
   M5 physics / title / joystick-1-only. BROWN ground. 20 Hz sim. No
   full-screen copy.

Crash-speed still resets to the pad (no explosion art). Aboard hostages are
counted killed on that reset, as in the original.

Ground colour stays BROWN. Video `stosw` is 44.8 ms/screen on hardware (M1);
this program never fills a page.

## Building

Same toolchain as [docs/M1.md](M1.md). From PowerShell:

```
. .\setenv.ps1
cmd /c "wmake NASM=lib\repos\nasm\nasm-3.02\nasm.exe"
```

That builds `build\m1.exe` through `build\m6.exe`. There is no `wmake run-m6`;
do not launch DOSBox from the makefile for this visual.

`GROUND=BROWN` (default) or `GROUND=PINK`. Clean first when changing it.

Hostage RLE is generated by `python tools/build_sprites.py` into
`src/sprdata_host.c` (chopper RLE stays in `src/sprdata_rle.c`, scenery in
`src/sprdata_world.c`).

## How to play (attended)

A human has to watch. The escape hatch is **Esc** (title → DOS, play → DOS).
Do not start an emulator until someone is in front of it.

### Real PCjr

`DEVICE=JRCONFIG.SYS /V64 /L`, pages 6 and 7. Copy `build\m6.exe`. Stick on
**joystick 1**. No `/force`.

```
M6
```

Centre the stick, SPACE to fly. **Up is thrust.** Fly **left** from the pad
past the fence to the four barracks. Eight hostages are already in the field
by the far (right-most) house; more come out of that burning house as you
load them.

1. **Land** among them (skids down, not a crash-speed hit). They run to the
   helicopter and board. HUD **A** counts up, cap **16**.
2. **Take off** and fly **right** home.
3. **Land on the base pad** (the same pad you started on). They hop out and
   run to the door. HUD **R** counts up as each one unloads.

`/force` on an emulator whose model byte is not `FDh`. `/batch` skips the
title; `/frames=N` caps retraces. **K** then WASD if there is no stick.

### What a pass looks like

- Title then chopper on the **base pad**, facing right, HUD `K00 A00 R00`.
- Barracks hostages visible as 8×11 white figures, waving / running.
- Land in the field: they run to the skids and **A** rises (not past 16).
- Land on the pad with a load: they run to the door and **R** rises.
- Camera lead 80/240, mountains slower than scenery, BROWN ground.
- No trail, no flicker, no full-screen flash on scroll.
- A hard landing at crash speed puts you back on the pad; **A** goes to 0
  and **K** absorbs whoever was aboard.

A fail is hostages that ignore a landed heli, boarding with no cap,
unload that never ticks R, a full-screen copy, or Esc not restoring DOS.

## What this does not do

- Joystick is still “works, not perfect”; this milestone does not retune it.
- Intact barracks do not empty (M7 tanks start the fires).
- No tanks, jets, saucers, bullets, or explosion / sink cycle (M7).
- No SN76496 (M8). No BCD bubbles / sortie banners / win-lose (M9).
- Crush has no dying-hostage sprite.
- Playdate rope is out of scope.
