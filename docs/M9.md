# M9 — presentation

`DESIGN.md` section 13: HUD, title, sortie banners, difficulty progression,
win and lose. This program does that and stops. Not hardware polish (M10).
Not the attract-loop demo (section 15 drops it on purpose). Title PVM
music is still optional and not here.

M8 put SN76496 effects on M7 combat. M9 is that game plus the original's
presentation layer. Attended runs open on the same BIOS mode 3 title as
M5–M8 (Joystick / Calibrate / Keyboard), then mode 8 logos, then play.

1. **HUD bubbles.** Rows 0–7, outside the dirty lists. Three **24×8** black
   wells on a light-green band, each with two **5×7** white digits (killed,
   aboard, rescued). New art, not a conversion of the Apple 43×9 bubble or
   6×7 font (`DESIGN.md` sections 6 and 15). Painted at sortie start and
   when a counter changes. F1 / `` ` `` still toggles the M5 debug line.
2. **Title logos.** After SPACE from the BIOS menu: Broderbund Presents,
   Choplifter logo, Dan Gorlin, Your Mission: Rescue Hostages. Converted
   even-X RLE from `titleGraphicsTable` (`src/sprdata_title.c`). SPACE
   skips one screen; Esc quits to DOS. No slide-in clip, no attract loop.
3. **Sortie banners.** FIRST / SECOND / THIRD SORTIE from
   `sortieGraphicsTable`, centred at the original bottom-relative `$6B`.
   Retriggered on each new life.
4. **Win / lose.** 64 rescued: Broderbund crown for 32 sim ticks, then the
   BIOS title. 64 killed or a third crash: The End, then title. Difficulty
   (`CURR_LEVEL` on pad unload) is unchanged from M7.
5. **Reuse.** M8 sound, JrConfig pages, `blit_rle`, BROWN ground, 20 Hz
   sim. No full-screen copy in the play loop. Title screens fill bands
   once; that is not a frame-budget path.

Ground colour stays BROWN. Video `stosw` is 44.8 ms/screen on hardware (M1);
the sim loop never fills a page.

## Current status

**Compiling increment.** `build\m9.exe` is 64,680 bytes. Attended pass:
logos then bubbles on the HUD; FIRST SORTIE art; crown / The End on a
finished run.

This program does **not** time dirty-rect or blit byte counts. Emulator
timings are a smoke test. DOSBox does not require the port `61h` gate; a
real PCjr does. Do not treat an emulator clock as hardware.

Quoted from `DESIGN.md` section 2 (real PCjr, `J:\GAMES\CHOPLIJR\M1.LOG`):
video `stosw` is **2803 ns/byte, 44.8 ms/screen**. A 16,000-byte fill or
copy does not fit a 50 ms 20 Hz frame.

## Building

Same toolchain as [docs/M1.md](M1.md). From PowerShell:

```
. .\setenv.ps1
cmd /c "wmake NASM=lib\repos\nasm\nasm-3.02\nasm.exe"
```

That builds `build\m1.exe` through `build\m9.exe`. There is no `wmake run-m9`;
do not launch DOSBox from the makefile for this visual.

`GROUND=BROWN` (default) or `GROUND=PINK`. Clean first when changing it.

Title / sortie / win-lose art is generated by `python tools/build_sprites.py`
into `src/sprdata_title.c`. HUD digits and the 24×8 wells are authored in
`src/m9.c`.

## Title and controls

Same as [docs/M5.md](M5.md): Joystick default, **C** calibrate, **K** WASD
help, SPACE to fly, Esc quits. After SPACE, four logo screens; SPACE skips
one. Joystick 1 only. Keyboard WASD / `.` rotate / `/` fire. Stick button 0
fires (airborne, cap 5). **Ctrl-S** mutes.

---

## How to play (attended)

A human has to watch **and listen**. The escape hatch is **Esc** (title or
logos → DOS, play → DOS). Do not start an emulator until someone is in front
of it.

Use `conf/pcjr-128k-m9.conf` (`m9.exe /force`). A pass is: BIOS title, then
the four logos, then the chopper on the pad with three HUD bubbles; FIRST
SORTIE art for ~2 s; a finished run shows the crown or The End and returns
to the BIOS title.

Emulator audio and frame times are a smoke test only.
