# M4 — the world

`DESIGN.md` section 13: scrolling, camera lead, mountain parallax, ground,
barracks, base, fence. This program does that and stops. It is not joystick
or the 11-step tilt machine (M5), not hostages, and not the game.

M3 put the flying chopper on `blit_rle` and per-buffer dirty lists. M4 is
that chopper riding the original world:

1. **Camera lead** from section 4: `SCROLL_LEAD_L` = 80, `SCROLL_LEAD_R` =
   240 (the Apple 70/210 pair scaled 320/280). A stub chopper flies left from
   the base to the far barracks and back; no joystick. Within the lead window
   the camera stays put, then it tracks.
2. **Scroll-invariant bands.** Sky and BROWN ground (plus the yellow
   highlight row) are painted once. They are not paid again when the camera
   moves. HUD lives in rows 0–7 and is never dirtied.
3. **Mountain strip.** On a scroll change for *this* back buffer, `fill_band`
   of 4 rows × 80 bytes (320 bytes — DESIGN.md section 7), not a 16,000-byte
   copy. Then the four CHOPGFX ridge sprites tile across at half rate.
4. **Scenery.** Intact barracks + sill (four houses at `FARHOUSE_X` + 256),
   five fence towers with the original parallax spread, base building, flagpole,
   two flag frames. Even/odd RLE from `tools/build_sprites.py` →
   `src/sprdata_world.c`. Dirty restores stay **even** byte widths.

The 6845 start-address registers stay in the optimisation drawer (section 7:
one memory word is coarser than 1–2 screen pixels per frame).

## Current status

**Accepted** (attended visual). Owner: scrolling world looks correct; the
demo is slower than real gameplay, accepted for this milestone. `build\m4.exe`
is 27,026 bytes. M5 adds the stick on this world.

This program does **not** time dirty-rect or blit byte counts. Section 14
still lists that measurement as the way to replace the analysis working-set
line. Do not treat an emulator clock as hardware.

Quoted from `DESIGN.md` section 2 (real PCjr, `J:\GAMES\CHOPLIJR\M1.LOG`):
video `stosw` is **2803 ns/byte, 44.8 ms/screen**; RAM→video `movsw` is
**62.9 ms/screen**. A 16,000-byte fill or copy does not fit a 50 ms 20 Hz
frame. M4 writes the mountain strip, word-padded dirty rects, and opaque
sprite bytes, never a full page.

Ground colour on this build is BROWN.

## Building

Same toolchain as [docs/M1.md](M1.md). From PowerShell:

```
. .\setenv.ps1
cmd /c "wmake NASM=lib\repos\nasm\nasm-3.02\nasm.exe"
```

That builds `build\m1.exe` through `build\m4.exe`. There is no `wmake run-m4`;
do not launch DOSBox from the makefile for this visual.

`GROUND=BROWN` (default) or `GROUND=PINK`. Clean first when changing it.

Scenery RLE is generated by `python tools/build_sprites.py` into
`src/sprdata_world.c` (chopper RLE stays in `src/sprdata_rle.c` so `m3.exe`
does not carry the world set).

## Running (attended)

A human has to watch. The escape hatch is a keypress. Do not start an
emulator until someone is in front of it.

When someone is watching, from a PCjr DOSBox-X with `build\` mounted as `C:`
(the existing `conf/pcjr-dev.conf` / `conf/pcjr-128k.conf` mounts, after M1
returns to the prompt, or by typing at `C:`):

```
M4
```

`/force` on an emulator whose model byte is not `FDh`. `/batch` skips the
"press a key" pause. `/frames=N` changes the motion length (default 2400).
`/bios` flips with `int 10h AX=0583h`. `/pa` `/pb` force pages in 1–7 — do
not overlay DOS-occupied pages on jrIDE.

On real hardware: the same `build\m4.exe`, JrConfig `DEVICE=JRCONFIG.SYS /V64 /L`,
pages 6 and 7. No `/force`.

### What a pass looks like

Text: memory / JrConfig / pages, then a key to enter mode 8 (unless
`/batch`). Then:

- Start at the **base**: light-grey building, white flagpole, red flag, HUD
  grey band on rows 0–7, BROWN ground with a yellow highlight row.
- Chopper sits **right of centre** (lead 240 world px = screen x 120) and
  flies left. The camera holds until the chopper reaches the left lead
  (screen x 40), then the world scrolls.
- **Fence** towers recede toward the horizon, then **four barracks** on the
  far side. Mountain silhouettes drift **slower** than the scenery.
- Turns around at the far house and flies home. Pose cycle is still M3's
  11 side + 5 head-on.
- **No trail**, **no flicker**, no venetian-blind bank error, HUD never
  overwritten by the playfield.

A fail is a full-screen flash each scroll step, mountains locked 1:1 with
the camera, houses sliding on the HUD, crumbs behind sprites, or a camera
that stays glued to the chopper (no lead).

Any key ends the motion early. After mode 3 is restored, the program prints
the pass-by-eye sentence and exits.

## What this does not do

- No joystick, no tilt state machine, no 20 Hz physics tables (M5).
- No hostages, fire, sidewalks, or grass-pad fill (later scenery / M6).
- No CRTC start-address pan (section 7 parks it).
- No Zen-timer of dirty-rect or `blit_rle` byte counts.
- Playdate is out of scope.
